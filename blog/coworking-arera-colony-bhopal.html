Coworking Space in Arera Colony, Bhopal —
Everything You Need to Know

If you live or work in Bhopal, chances are Arera Colony is already familiar to you. It's one of the city's most established and well-connected neighbourhoods — home to professionals, businesses, cafés, banks, hospitals, and a pace of life that suits people who take their work seriously. For freelancers, consultants, and growing teams who want a workspace that feels right, Arera Colony is often the first place they look.

And now, Oneworx has not one but two coworking centres here — giving you more options, more flexibility, and more reasons to make Arera Colony your professional home base.

Why Work in Arera Colony?

Arera Colony isn't just a residential area — it's one of Bhopal's most active commercial and professional zones. The neighbourhood has everything a working professional needs within walking distance or a short drive.

Excellent Connectivity

Well-connected to MP Nagar, Bittan Market, New Market, and the rest of Bhopal via main roads. Easy to reach from both sides of the city.

Restaurants & Cafés

From quick lunch spots to sit-down restaurants and coffee shops — Arera Colony has a strong dining scene that makes long work days far more enjoyable.

Banks & Services

Multiple banks, ATMs, courier services, print shops, and pharmacies are nearby — so your errands never have to interrupt your day.

Professional Neighbourhood

A credible business address in Arera Colony carries weight with clients, partners, and on your invoices — whether you're a solo consultant or a growing team.

Oneworx Arera Colony — Our Two Centres

Oneworx currently operates two coworking centres in Arera Colony — our Arera Colony centres each have their own character, and together they give you the most workspace coverage in the neighbourhood.

OWE5 — E-5, Arera Colony

First Floor, 43 B, near Vandematram Square, E-5, Arera Colony

Our E-5 centre is Oneworx's flagship location in Arera Colony — designed for professionals who prefer a quieter, more composed work environment. The space prioritises comfort and clarity: calm interiors, ergonomic furniture, consistent high-speed internet, and a well-organised setup that makes it easy to focus.

It's ideal for consultants and independent professionals who need a steady, reliable base — and for small teams that want privacy and a professional atmosphere without the overhead of a standalone office.

What's available at OWE5:

  • • Private Cabins — enclosed, lockable, ideal for small teams
  • • Private Offices — fully furnished, move-in ready

OW99 — 10 No. Market, Arera Colony

Second Floor, Aastha Arcade, 10 No. Market, E-4, Arera Colony

OW99 at 10 No. Market is Oneworx's newest centre — a modern, design-forward workspace in one of Bhopal's most vibrant commercial hubs. Situated close to cafés, retail outlets, and key city access points, this location blends work and lifestyle seamlessly.

It's the spot for creative professionals, client-facing teams, and anyone who wants a workspace that feels current, energetic, and well-located for both meetings and daily work.

What's available at OW99:

  • • Dedicated Desks — your own fixed desk, leave your setup overnight
  • • Private Cabins — enclosed team spaces
  • • Private Offices — for growing teams
  • • Meeting Rooms — equipped with displays, whiteboards & Wi-Fi
  • • Day Passes — drop in for the day from 9 AM to 7 PM

Who Works Here?

Our Arera Colony members are a mix of people — all with different work styles, but a shared preference for a good environment and a neighbourhood that works for them.

Freelancers & Consultants

Professionals who work independently and need a reliable daily base — with fast internet, a quiet atmosphere, and a proper desk that isn't their dining table.

Remote Employees

Working for companies based in other cities or countries — they need a structured workspace with professional amenities to stay productive and connected across time zones.

Startups & Small Teams

Early-stage teams that need an enclosed, professional space to collaborate and build — without the cost and commitment of a standalone office in Arera Colony.

Businesses Needing an Address

Companies that need a professional Arera Colony address for GST registration, MCA filing, or client communication — without renting a full physical space.
